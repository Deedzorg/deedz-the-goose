# Gameplay Mechanics Reference

Use these names when discussing balance, controls, bugs, or new features. The parenthetical phrase describes the mechanic's intended role.

## Movement

- **Ground Move** — standard left/right movement.
- **Crouch-Walk** — slower movement while holding down, with a shorter collision profile.
- **Triple Flap** — the three-stage jump sequence.
- **Glide Hold** — holding Flap after the third flap to stay airborne longer.
- **Dash** — the fast horizontal burst with a short recovery window.
- **Updraft Lift** — a world current that carries geese upward.
- **Trampoline Bounce** — automatic launch from a trampoline platform.

## Combat and actions

- **Power Peck** — a heavy single-target beak strike that lunges forward, deals three damage, and launches ordinary enemies backward.
- **Wing Whap** — wider melee cleave that can hit several enemies and has the strongest close-range knockback.
- **Charged Crumb Throw** — hold-and-release ranged attack that spends one throwable crumb.
- **Power Crumb** — a nearly full Charged Crumb Throw that deals two damage.
- **Honk Pulse** — radial utility attack that knocks enemies back, awakens objects, and breaks boss shields.
- **Flock Sense** — held scan that reveals nearby objectives, enemies, collectibles, and geese.
- **Wing-Bump** — contextual multiplayer interaction with a nearby goose.
- **Fox Stomp** — landing on a Bread Fox from above instantly defeats it and bounces the goose back into the air.
- **Hazard Knockout** — pushing a Bread Fox into water, thorns, or an open fall defeats it.

On phones, the visible action set is **Flap, Wing Whap, Peck, Crumb, Honk, and Dash**. Flock Sense and Wing-Bump remain available on keyboard/controller but are intentionally omitted from the compact touch HUD.

## World objectives and progression

- **Echo Crystal Awakening** — Honk an Echo Crystal to activate it.
- **Echo Resonator** — renewable Honk target that grants progression resources.
- **Goose Post** — checkpoint and recovery location.
- **Collectible Trail** — route of crumbs and other pickups through a level.
- **Echo XP** — personal experience used to reach the next Echo Layer.
- **Boss Charge** — shared meter introduced in Echo Layer 2; summons Breadstorm at full charge.
- **Breadstorm Crisis** — the active Baron Breadstorm encounter in Moonwater Ravine, near the center of the map.
- **Breadstorm Hearts** — Form 1 has one heart, Form 2 has two, and each later form adds one more successful hit.
- **Echo Shield** — opening boss defense introduced in Echo Layer 5. Honk it before ordinary attacks can remove hearts.
- **Phase Shield** — renewed mid-fight shield introduced in Echo Layer 7, after players have learned the simpler forms.
- **Echo Layer Evolution** — the world transformation reached at Foxfire Gate. Layer 1 has no boss requirement; later layers require their Breadstorm victory.
- **Echo Supply** — persistent crumb ammunition and other carried resources.
- **Fox Crumb Drop** — defeated scouts, guards, and captains drop two, three, or four collectible crumbs.
- **Recoverable Crumb Spill** — a successful Crumb-Snatch Bat hit knocks loose up to three throwable crumbs. They arc out of Deedz and can be collected again without granting repeat progression.
- **Flock Score** — score used for connected-player ranking.

## Enemy archetypes

- **Bread Charger** — direct ground pursuer.
- **Pounce Fox** — mobile enemy that commits to leaping attacks.
- **Crumb Lobber** — ranged enemy that pressures from a distance.
- **Toast Guard** — sturdy brute that controls space.
- **Ice-Slide Penguin** — Echo 10 ground enemy that telegraphs a fast sliding charge.
- **Crumb-Snatch Bat** — Echo 10 flying enemy that winds up, swoops, and knocks loose recoverable crumbs only when its attack connects.
- **Echo Captain** — stronger elite enemy.
- **Baron Breadstorm** — evolving shared boss introduced with one heart and no shield in Echo Layer 2. Same-Echo geese share the fight; opening shields begin at Echo 5 and phase shields at Echo 7.

## Evolving-world features

- **Echo Layer** — the current world difficulty and evolution tier.
- **Layer Mutation** — a rule change applied as the world evolves.
- **Moving Platform**, **Cloud Platform**, and **Phase Platform** — traversal platform families.
- **Enemy Roster Evolution** — new enemy archetypes joining the spawn pool in later layers.
- **Adaptive Echo Music** — music arrangement that changes by layer and boss intensity.

## Character terminology

- **Goose Class** — gameplay stat profile and special trait.
- **Eight-Goose Roster** — Silly, Happy Happy, Crumb, Curvy, Sweet, Bad Bad, Fun Size, and Ember Goose, each with a distinct silhouette and signature detail.
- **Goose Unlock Challenges** — Silly Goose, Happy Happy Goose, and Crumb Goose start unlocked. Collect 50 crumbs for Sweet Goose, defeat 20 enemies for Bad Bad Goose, awaken 3 Echo Crystals for Fun Size Goose, defeat Breadstorm once for Ember Goose, and reach Echo 10 for Curvy Goose.
- **Random Goose Name** — the dice button beside the profile name creates a new playful name and saves it immediately.
- **Top Goose** — the main-menu honor names the highest-scoring goose among the local save and connected room players.
- **Plumage** — cosmetic goose color; no stat effect.
- **Deedz** — the default goose name, not a mechanic or game title.

Good future requests include: “increase Power Peck reach,” “change Fox Stomp bounce,” “make Crumb-Snatch Bats appear one layer earlier,” or “add a Layer Mutation after the second Breadstorm Crisis.”
